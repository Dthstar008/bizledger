# BizLedger: 30-Day Instagram Content Plan

**Goal:** Grow a real waitlist of Nigerian small-business owners and learn what they struggle with.
**Primary CTA all month:** "Join the waitlist: link in bio."
**Handle:** @bizledger.ng

## Targets (realistic for a new account, adjust as you learn)
| Metric | Day 30 target |
|---|---|
| Waitlist sign-ups | 100 |
| Followers | 500 |
| Reel average views | 1,000+ |
| Saves + shares per carousel | 20+ |
| Merchant conversations (DM/WhatsApp) | 15 |

Sign-ups matter most. Followers matter least. Check the Supabase `waitlist` table weekly.

## Content pillars
| Pillar | Share | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 💡 Money tips | 35% | Useful, saveable, builds trust |
| 😂 Relatable skits and memes | 25% | Reach: shop-life moments people share |
| 📱 Product peeks | 20% | Show BizLedger working (demo data only) |
| 🛠️ Build in public | 15% | Founder story, student engineer building for traders |
| 🤝 Community | 5% | Polls, Q&As, shout-outs |

## Weekly rhythm
- **Mon:** Carousel (tip or education)
- **Tue:** Stories (poll or question)
- **Wed:** Reel (skit or demo)
- **Thu:** Build-in-public post or Story
- **Fri:** Reel or meme
- **Sat:** Community / Q&A
- **Sun:** Rest, or one Story. Plan next week.

Post 18:00-20:00 WAT on weekdays, around 11:00 on weekends. Reply to every comment within the first hour.

## Rules for this account
1. **Honest claims only.** Say "waitlist" or "early access". Don't mention offline mode, receipts or suppliers: those are Stage 2.
2. **Demo data only** in screen recordings (the seeded demo merchant). Never real customers.
3. **No fake testimonials, user counts or revenue.** Real numbers or none.
4. **Never promise returns or give investment advice.** Tips are bookkeeping basics.

## Caption template
1. **Hook** (first line, under 10 words, a question or bold statement)
2. **Value** (2-4 short lines, simple English, a little Pidgin is fine)
3. **Soft CTA** ("Join the waitlist: link in bio")
4. **Engagement question** (one question)
5. Hashtags go in the **first comment** (8-12)

## Hashtag bank (rotate, don't repeat all)
`#BizLedger #NigerianBusiness #SmallBusinessNigeria #SME #Bookkeeping #BusinessTips #NaijaBusiness #MoneyManagement #Entrepreneur #Fintech #MadeInNigeria #OwoOndo #TraderLife #ShopOwner #LagosBusiness #SupportSmallBusiness`

## Daily 20-minute routine
- 5 min: reply to comments and DMs
- 5 min: comment on 10 posts from traders, SME pages and Nigerian tech accounts
- 5 min: post or schedule today's item
- 5 min: add anything useful (a question, a quote) to the content bank

## Weekly review (Sunday, 15 minutes)
1. Count new waitlist rows in Supabase
2. Top 3 posts by saves and shares: make more like them
3. Bottom 3 posts: drop that format
4. Read DMs and comments for new post ideas
5. Adjust next week's rows
